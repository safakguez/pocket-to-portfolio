From pocket money to portfolio

You start at 15, living with your parents – with pocket money and side jobs like delivering newspapers, walking dogs or washing cars. Work your way up through an apprenticeship or college, move into a shared flat, your own apartment or a house – and learn what really matters in real life:

  • Saving and an emergency fund for the unexpected
  • Investing in a broadly diversified ETF and the power of compound interest
  • Spotting debt traps: overdrafts, installments, lifestyle inflation
  • Gross vs. net pay, rent and budgeting

A simplified game simulation, not investment advice. For players aged 13 and up.
